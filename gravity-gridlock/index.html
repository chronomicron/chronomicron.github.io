    QUICK BRIEFING

    How to play

    1. 01
      Set up & turn the arena

      Choose distinct bubble origins in Board setup; each target is the other player's origin. Bottom-left rotates clockwise and bottom-right counter-clockwise, up to twice before your swap. Rotation moves bubbles and targets together; gravity stays vertical on screen.

    2. 02
      Swap for points or move a bubble toward a goal

      Either bubble may swap with a neighboring tile on the active player's turn, but the swap must make a match. The two bubbles cannot swap with each other. The active bubble may also swap directly into its adjacent, tile-occupied goal without a match; that awards the board-side bonus and ends the round. Each unique tile cleared scores 1 point for the active player.

    3. 03
      Set the turn flow

      Mercury turns flow down and Air turns flow up, regardless of board rotation. Both bubbles move with the ordered lane contents and cannot pass each other. Walls stay fixed; tiles flow past their rows, but walls stop bubbles.

    4. 04
      Settle & cascade

      Cleared gaps compact across walls toward the active turn's flow. Remaining empty cells refill from the upstream board edge. Every cascade scores for the same active player.

    5. 05
      Hint & recover

      Hint shows a legal matching swap and its match cells for 1 point (your score cannot drop below 0). If the settled board has no legal move, tile colors shuffle while bubbles, walls, and goals stay put.

    6. 06
      Optional turn timer

      The host can choose 30 seconds or 1 minute in Board setup. If time runs out, a random legal move is made, including a possible rotation.

    ✦

    Either bubble reaching its own goal ends the round and awards a bonus equal to the board side length. Highest score wins; the arriving player wins a tie.
