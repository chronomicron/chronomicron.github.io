# Noto Emoji assets

The 12 transparent PNG icons in this directory are 128 × 128 conversions of the corresponding SVG artwork from Google's Noto Emoji v2.047 collection.

The artwork is copyright Google and licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. See [LICENSE](./LICENSE) and the [upstream Noto Emoji SVG collection](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/tree/v2.047/svg). PNGs are converted with CairoSVG; source SVGs are not bundled. The project's tile color indices and match rules remain independent of these visual assets.
