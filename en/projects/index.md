# Projects

I work on a wide range of projects, from larger software applications to small hardware experiments and electronics repairs.

Many of the things I do feel small or routine to me — quick scripts, board-level fixes, restoring an old console or radio, debugging a sensor — so they often never get written up formally. Still, they form an important part of how I spend my time: understanding how things work, keeping older equipment alive, and reducing electronic waste whenever possible.

The pages in this section highlight some of the more complete or ongoing efforts, grouped into **Software Applications** and **Hardware Applications**.
